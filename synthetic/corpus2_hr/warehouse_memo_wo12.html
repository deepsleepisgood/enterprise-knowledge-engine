Memo WO-12: Leave notice during peak season (Warehouse Operations)

Issued1 September 2026
FromHead of Warehouse Operations
Applies toWarehouse Operations staff only

From 1 October to 31 December each year, Warehouse Operations staff must request annual leave at least 30 days before the first day of leave. This replaces the general notice period for this department during that season only. All other leave rules are unchanged.
